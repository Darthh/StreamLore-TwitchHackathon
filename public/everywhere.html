How it works

Once StreamLore is on:

  1. 1

    Watch as usual

    Every few minutes StreamLore checks which channels you follow are live and reads their chat as you.

  2. 2

    Ask in chat

    Type a question such as “whats a juicer”, “what does Sally mean”, or “!lore juicer”.

  3. 3

    Get a Whisper

    streamlore26 whispers you the meaning and a source. If you block Whispers from strangers, follow streamlore26 first.

Answers are sent from streamlore26, which must be phone-verified on Twitch. The account owner connects it once; nobody else can complete this sign-in.
