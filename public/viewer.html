Enable it once. Take it with you.

  1. Install the personal browser add-on.

    Download and unzip the development build. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.

    Download the viewer add-onThis is an unpacked Chrome/Chromium build. It has not been published to the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Connect your own Twitch account.

    Open StreamLore from the browser toolbar and connect. This identifies the account that should receive your Whispers.

    Connect my Twitch account ↗
  3. Ask in the chat you’re watching.

    Try “whats a juicer?” in xQc’s chat or “What is Sally?” in Caedrel’s. !lore <phrase> also works. Enable lore Whispers in the add-on.

Private replies. Known definitions.

The add-on detects your own submitted questions and the current channel. It doesn’t read other viewers’ messages or your private Whisper history. Definitions come from sourced starter references or approved channel lore. Unknown phrases stay unknown.

Whispers come from the connected StreamLore bot. Twitch requires a phone-verified sender, can filter delivery, and limits the sender to 40 unique recipients per day. A request accepted by Twitch does not prove that a Whisper arrived.
